Technology Behind 360 Hologram Display

Projection Technology

Ring Projector Array

Ring-mounted 4-6 projectors provide complete 360-degree coverage from all angles. Advanced geometry mapping ensures content appears identical regardless of viewing position. Multi-projector content server synchronizes all projectors to frame-level accuracy.
