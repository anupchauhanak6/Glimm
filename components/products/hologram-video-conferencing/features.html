Technology Behind Hologram Video Conferencing

Telepresence Tech

3D Volumetric Capture

Multi-camera depth sensing captures complete 3D point clouds of remote participants in real-time. Advanced AI removes background and optimizes lighting for realistic holographic rendering. Proprietary codec reduces 3D data to streaming-friendly size while maintaining fidelity. Eye-tracking and gaze correction algorithms ensure natural eye contact improving non-verbal communication and participant engagement across global distances.
