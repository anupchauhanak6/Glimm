Technology Behind Hologram in Air

Plasma Technology

Volumetric Plasma Display

High-powered lasers focus at precise 3D coordinates ionizing air molecules to create glowing plasma voxels. Multi-wavelength laser array produces full RGB color gamut with synchronized timing control for smooth animation. Advanced beam-steering optics redirect lasers thousands of times per second to paint volumetric imagery in mid-air. Safety sensors monitor laser power ensuring eye-safe operation while maintaining visual brightness for immersive experiences.
