Transparent LED Architecture

Ultra-thin LED chips mount on transparent substrate material maintaining 70-85% light transmission with advanced optics focusing light forward while minimizing sideways scatter. Proprietary pixel architecture balances color saturation with see-through capability at multiple viewing angles. Embedded heat-dissipation layers prevent LED degradation during continuous operation with automatic brightness reduction in high ambient heat.
