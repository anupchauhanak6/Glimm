Window Hologram Overview

Revolutionary window-projected hologram technology transforms retail storefronts into immersive 3D experience centers. Customers see transparent floating holograms while maintaining clear visibility of the store interior.

Ideal Use Cases

  • Premium fashion and luxury boutique storefronts
  • Electronics and tech retail flagship locations
  • Shopping mall anchor stores seeking differentiation

Why Choose Window Holograms?

  • Retail Windows - Transform storefronts
  • Storefronts - Attract foot traffic
  • Shopping Malls - Stand out from competition
  • Showrooms - Create immersive brand experiences
  • Seamless glass integration
  • Maintains interior visibility

Key Features

Seamless Glass Integration

Works with standard retail glass without special materials or modifications required

Interior Visibility

Maintains 75-85% transparency allowing customers to see through to store interior

Daylight Viewing
