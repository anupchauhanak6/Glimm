Projection Technology

Optical Engineering

Light-Bending Projection Gauze

Advanced polyester weave technology creates precise light diffusion patterns. The micro-engineered fiber structure allows projector light to scatter and refract creating true 3D holographic illusions while maintaining 95% optical clarity for background visibility.

How It Works:

  • Micro-fiber weave pattern
  • Selective light diffusion
  • Zero ghosting effect
  • 360° viewing angles
