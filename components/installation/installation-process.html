Our Process

Professional 5-Step Installation Process

From initial consultation to final handover, we ensure seamless deployment with minimal disruption to your operations

01

Site Assessment & Planning

Our technical team conducts a comprehensive on-site evaluation including structural analysis, power requirements, network infrastructure assessment, and ambient lighting conditions. We create detailed installation blueprints and timeline projections.

  • Structural load calculations
  • Power & network requirements
  • Environmental analysis
  • Timeline & logistics planning
Duration: 1-2 days
02

Equipment Preparation & Logistics

All equipment is thoroughly inspected, tested, and prepared at our facility before transport. We coordinate delivery schedules, secure necessary permits, and arrange specialized equipment transportation with climate-controlled vehicles when required.

  • Pre-installation testing
  • Quality control inspection
  • Secure packaging & transport
  • Permit coordination
Duration: 3-5 days
03

Physical Installation & Mounting

Certified technicians handle the physical installation following strict safety protocols. We perform structural mounting, secure all hardware, implement professional cable management, and ensure load distribution meets or exceeds safety standards.

  • Precision mounting & alignment
  • Professional cable management
  • Safety compliance verification
  • Hardware stress testing
Duration: 1-5 days
04

System Integration & Calibration

Complete system integration with your existing infrastructure including network configuration, content management system setup, color calibration, brightness optimization, and audio synchronization for multimedia displays.

  • Network & software integration
  • Display calibration & optimization
  • Content system configuration
  • Performance testing
Duration: 1-2 days
05

Training & Handover

Comprehensive training for your team on system operation, content management, basic troubleshooting, and maintenance procedures. We provide detailed documentation, emergency contact protocols, and schedule follow-up support visits.

  • Hands-on operational training
  • Content management workshop
  • Complete documentation package
  • 24/7 support activation
Duration: 1 day

Industry-Leading Warranty & Support

All installations include a comprehensive 2-year warranty covering both equipment and workmanship. Our 24/7 technical support team is available for immediate assistance, and we offer preventive maintenance programs to ensure optimal performance.
